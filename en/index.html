Autooria — Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective 10 October 2026

1. General information

Autooria is a car manager developed by Autooria / Piotr Kuraś. It stores your car records on your device and does not require an account. This policy explains how the app handles data and how the developer handles information you voluntarily send by email. You do not need to accept this policy to use Autooria.

2. Data stored locally

Car details, including VIN, registration number and name, fuel, expense, service, mileage and reminder records, amounts and notes are stored in the private app database on your device. Settings are also stored locally. The dashboard and statistics are calculated on your device. Photos restored from a backup are saved in private app files; a photo referenced outside the app remains with its original provider. Autooria does not provide cloud synchronization of your car history.

Records remain until you delete them or replace them by importing a backup. You can edit or delete records in the app. Android’s Clear storage or Clear data action removes the app’s local database, settings and private files, including restored photos. Clearing only the cache does not delete your records. Uninstalling Autooria also removes its private local data. Automatic Android backup and system transfer of Autooria app data are disabled. External photo originals and exported files are not removed by these actions; see section 9.

Private app storage uses Android’s application isolation and access protections. Autooria does not add its own database encryption. Protect access to your device; the protection of files held by external apps or providers depends on their safeguards.

3. Network access

The core features of Autooria work offline. The app does not connect to developer servers or automatically send car records or settings to the developer. Exporting a backup or sending an email through an external application is a separate action you choose. Those applications or file providers may use the Internet.

4. Notifications

Reminder notifications use Android’s local scheduling and notification mechanisms. Their availability depends on your system permission and notification settings and on Android/device restrictions. You can manage system notifications from Autooria Settings. Disabling notifications does not prevent you from using the app or managing reminders.

5. External services

Opening the contact email passes the address to your email application; Autooria does not send a message or attach your records automatically. For backup and restore, Android’s system file picker lets you choose a file or destination. External applications and providers handle information under their own privacy policies and security measures. Sections 7 and 9 explain email and exported files respectively. These services are not Autooria cloud synchronization.

6. Advertising and analytics

The current version of Autooria contains no advertising, user analytics or automatic crash-reporting service. It does not process car records using artificial intelligence (AI), recognize text in images (OCR) or access GPS location. This describes Autooria, not the separate practices of Android, your email app or a file provider you choose.

7. Contact

Developer and controller of personal data received in correspondence: Piotr Kuraś (Autooria), Poland.
Email: autooria.contact@gmail.com

You may voluntarily send questions, contact details, a problem description or attachments. Provide only information needed for your request. The developer receives what you send; sending an email does not give access to your local Autooria database. You can use the app without contacting the developer, but without sufficient information it may not be possible to answer your request.

Correspondence is used to answer questions and handle reported problems, based on the legitimate interest in handling these requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Requests to exercise GDPR rights are handled to fulfil a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). Messages are kept until the matter is resolved, and longer only where necessary to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend a specific legal claim. Once that need ends, the developer deletes the correspondence.

Messages are handled and stored through Gmail (Google) and the email services used by the sender. The developer does not disclose correspondence or copies of it to other persons unless disclosure is required by applicable law. Google may process correspondence outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Google describes its transfer safeguards, including adequacy decisions, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, where applicable, standard contractual clauses, at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks. Its privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Provider retention and deletion processes also apply; deletion from the developer’s mailbox does not guarantee immediate deletion of all provider copies.

For data the developer holds, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing, subject to the conditions in GDPR. You may object to legitimate-interest processing for reasons relating to your particular situation. Data portability applies where its legal conditions are met; it does not normally apply to correspondence based on legitimate interest or a legal obligation. Contact the email above to exercise your rights. You may complain to the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, https://uodo.gov.pl) or another competent supervisory authority.

8. Policy changes

The policy may be updated when the behaviour of Autooria changes. The version and effective date are shown on this screen. The policy is included in the app and is readable without an Internet connection.

9. Manual backup and restore

Autooria supports manual export and import of .autooria backups. Export happens only when you choose to create a backup. You choose the destination or source with Android’s system file picker (Storage Access Framework); the app receives access only to the file you select. You may choose local storage or an external provider shown by Android, such as Google Drive or OneDrive. A provider may store the file outside your device under its own privacy policy and security measures. Autooria does not upload the backup to its own cloud and does not provide automatic cloud synchronization.

Deleting records, clearing app data or uninstalling Autooria does not delete exported backups. Delete those files separately in the location or provider you chose; external copies and their deletion remain under your and the provider’s control. A backup also includes recorded amounts. The file may contain VINs, registration numbers, vehicle history, notes, photos and other car details. Backup v1 is not password-encrypted. Keep it in a safe place.
